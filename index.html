Q1

Acute and maintenance trial design

Sponsor starting point

The proposed seamless Phase 2/3 study uses Stage 1 for adaptive dose finding, Stage 2 for pivotal acute efficacy, and Stage 3 for open-label maintenance with external controls. Stage 1 efficacy pooling is not assumed.

Clinician question

Do you approve this clinical-trial structure for evaluating acute treatment and maintenance, and what would you change?

Safety follow-up across stages

Q5

Human abuse-liability assessment content

Sponsor starting point

The proposed clinical assessment set is intended to cover subjective and dissociative effects, reinforcing effects, craving, misuse, diversion, and dependence or withdrawal signals; a dedicated human-abuse-potential study is retained as a fallback.

Clinician question

Do you approve this assessment content, and what would you change or add?

Q8

At-home treatment eligibility and oversight

Sponsor starting point

Home treatment is proposed only after review of safety, recovery, and competency, with clinician-selected eligibility and ongoing clinician oversight.

Q9

Telehealth and practical clinical management

Sponsor starting point

The digital-health and at-home-care concept is intended to support clinician-led follow-up while keeping clinical interpretation and action with healthcare professionals.

Clinician question

Q10

Digital follow-up and passive measures

Sponsor starting point

NDC-01 is described as passive data capture, protocol-timed prompting, unchanged display of patient and treatment data, recordkeeping, and clinician-initiated communication.

Clinician question

Do you approve these digital follow-up functions for clinical care, and what would you change?

Q11

Missing or unreliable digital data

Sponsor starting point

NDC-01 is not proposed to generate automated alerts, risk scores, or care recommendations; clinicians retain interpretation and action.

Clinician question

If digital data are missing or unreliable, do you approve a clinician-led manual fallback, and what should be required before treatment continues?

Q12

Patient selection and clinician oversight

Sponsor starting point

The clinician is intended to decide whether a participant is ready for home treatment and whether a caregiver is needed after safety, recovery, and competency review.

Clinician question

Do you approve this clinician-led selection and oversight model, and what decision points should be explicit?

Q13

Treatment completion and discharge

Sponsor starting point

The programme proposes objective discharge-readiness assessment, with driving clearance treated as a separate decision from clinical discharge.

Clinician question

Do you approve this separation of treatment completion, discharge, and driving decisions, and what should the discharge check include?

Q14

Driving safety after treatment

Sponsor starting point

The starting position is a 24-hour driving restriction after treatment, with clearance considered separately from discharge.

Clinician question

Do you approve the 24-hour restriction, and what would you change for safe real-world use?
